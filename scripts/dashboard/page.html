Definitions
Works
The test passed, or a documented gap no longer occurs.
Offered
Works, documented gap or new failure: the engine and the catalog have the capability, so the test ran.
Reliability
Works / offered. How well what is offered works.
Equal theme weight
Reliability per theme, then the mean over the themes. Without it, themes with large matrices (types: about a third of all cases) decide the total.
Scope
Works / all cases of the engine, counting "not offered" and its own "not run" as not working. How much of the whole test catalogue works.
Not offered
The engine or catalog has no API or type for it; checked by the lab (test_na_claims).
Not testable
A step before failed, mostly the engine that writes the table. The case counts at that step: against the engine that failed, or the catalog when it makes the state impossible - not against the engine that would have read. Whether the reader can read such a table shows the neutral source.
Planned / ran
Planned: every compared case. Ran: works, documented gap or new failure. The difference is "not offered" and "not run".
Severe gap
A documented gap that loses or corrupts data, gives wrong results, leaves a commit half applied or opens access wider than intended.
New failure
A failure without a documented gap, or with a different error than documented.

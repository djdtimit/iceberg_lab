Eight engines on four Iceberg REST catalogs (Lakekeeper v0.15.1, Apache Polaris 1.9.0, the built-in catalog of RustFS 1.0.6 and Unity Catalog OSS 0.7.0) with the same object storage (RustFS). Every engine runs the same test cases in every area and writes the starting data itself; what a catalog does not offer counts as "not offered". The numbers above, the overview, the single results and the gaps apply to the chosen catalog. Special features of single catalogs or engines, demo notebooks and lab self-tests do not count; they are listed separately further down.
